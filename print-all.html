Our Story

Making loans easier to understand

Platinum Finance Advisory was founded to solve a simple problem: loan terms are confusing, offers are hard to compare, and business owners often don't know whether a bank loan or a Private Funder actually fits their situation and timeline.

We act as an intermediary between borrowers and lenders — reviewing your needs, explaining your options in plain language, and presenting offers side by side so you can make an informed decision with confidence.

What Is a Loan Broker?

Broker vs. lender — what's the difference?

A lender (such as a bank or financial institution) is the entity that actually provides the loan funds. A broker, like Platinum Finance Advisory, does not lend money directly. Instead, we:

  • Assess your borrowing needs and financial situation
  • Compare suitable loan products across our lender network
  • Explain rates, fees, and terms in plain language
  • Support your application through to approval

Property-Backed Business Loans

Borrow against property you or your business already owns — usually the largest quantum and lowest rates available, in exchange for a longer, more document-heavy process.

Property-Backed

How it works

A property-backed business loan uses residential, commercial, or industrial property as collateral. Because the lender has a clear asset to recover against, this route typically unlocks larger loan amounts and materially lower interest rates than unsecured business financing — the trade-off is a longer approval timeline and more documentation.

Common uses

  • Business expansion or large capital projects
  • Debt consolidation onto a lower rate
  • Purchasing commercial or industrial premises

Typical structure

Max loan-to-value (LTV)Residential ~80% · Commercial/Industrial ~60%
TenorTypically 5 to 15 years
Pricing basisInterest-servicing, quoted p.a.

Actual LTV, rate and tenor depend on property type, valuation, and the specific lender — your advisor will confirm exact figures for your situation.

Advanced Option

Equity term loans

If you already own private property or an executive condominium past its Minimum Occupation Period, you may be able to borrow against the equity you've built up — without selling.

Who's eligible

  • Private residential property, or ECs past MOP
  • Commercial and industrial property owners
  • HDB flats are not eligible for equity term loans

What it's commonly used for

  • Funding renovations or major expenses
  • Supporting family or unlocking cash without a sale
  • Business or investment capital
Know the Rules

LTV, TDSR & MSR — the numbers that decide your loan

These three regulatory limits determine how much you can borrow before you even look at a specific lender's offer.

Loan-to-Value (LTV)

The maximum percentage of a property's value a lender will finance. The rest must come from your own funds — cash, CPF, or equity.

Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR)

All your monthly debt repayments — across every loan and credit facility — are capped at a set percentage of your gross monthly income. Existing debts reduce how much new financing you can take on.

Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR)

A stricter, property-specific limit applying to HDB flats and ECs: your property loan repayments alone are capped at a set percentage of gross monthly income.

⚠ A Word of Caution — Caveat Loans

You may come across "caveat loans" — fast cash secured by lodging a caveat on a property title, offered by Private Funders rather than banks. They can carry interest well above bank rates and are generally a last resort for borrowers who don't qualify for a standard bank or equity term loan.

If a caveat loan is ever relevant to your situation, we'll walk you through exactly what it means before you commit, and we always recommend exhausting standard bank options first and seeking independent legal advice before signing anything that puts your property title at risk.

Working Capital

Working Capital Loan

Short-to-medium-term funding to bridge day-to-day operational gaps — payroll timing, supplier deposits, or stocking up ahead of a busy season.

  • Fast assessment based on bank statement health
  • Shorter tenures than a standard term loan
  • Best for a known, temporary cash-flow gap
Trade

Trade Term Loan

A revolving trade facility that funds specific supplier or buyer transactions — draw down against an invoice, repay over a fixed schedule, and draw again as new orders come in.

  • Suited to import/export and wholesale trade
  • Financing tied to individual invoices or purchase orders
  • Facility can be reused across a 12-month term
Invoice Financing

Accounts Receivable Financing

Pledge your issued invoices and receive a large portion of their value upfront in cash, rather than waiting out your customer's payment terms.

  • Releases cash tied up in unpaid invoices
  • Especially useful when selling to large companies on long payment terms
  • Notified or non-notified structures available
How Invoice Financing Works

From invoice to cash in hand

1

You invoice your buyer

You deliver a product or service and issue an invoice on your usual payment terms.

2

Pledge the invoice

You assign the invoice to the financing provider as security for an advance.

3

Receive cash upfront

A significant portion of the invoice value is disbursed to you immediately.

4

Buyer pays, you're rebated

Once your buyer settles the invoice, you receive the remaining balance, minus fees.

Eligibility, generally

  • Singapore-registered business (Pte Ltd or LLP typically preferred)
  • Operating history — requirements vary by facility and lender, from a few months to 12+ months
  • Evidence of genuine trade activity: invoices, purchase orders, or supplier agreements

Exact quantum, tenor and pricing depend on the specific facility and lender — your advisor will confirm what applies to your business.

Personal

Personal Instalment Loan

A lump sum with a fixed monthly repayment, amortised evenly over a set term — typically 1 to 7 years. The most predictable option for planned expenses.

Restructuring

Debt Consolidation Plan

Combines multiple unsecured debts — credit cards and personal loans — into a single facility, usually at a lower rate than card interest. Well suited to anyone juggling several high-interest balances.

Flexible

Credit Line

A revolving facility you draw from as needed, paying interest only on the amount actually used. A practical backup for short-term liquidity rather than a single planned expense.

Purpose-Bound

Renovation Loan

Financing for HDB or condo renovation, usually disbursed directly to contractors rather than to you. Loan quantum is typically capped relative to your monthly income.

⚠ A Word of Caution — Payday & Micro-Loans

Very short-term "payday"-style micro-loans typically carry the highest rates of any personal credit product and are best treated as a last resort, not a first option. If one of the products above can meet your need, we'll always route you there first.

Who Qualifies

Eligibility requirements for bank business loans and Private Funder loans. Final eligibility is confirmed by the lender during assessment.

Bank

■ Bank business loans

  • Singapore-registered business (Pte Ltd / sole prop)
  • At least 2 years in operation
  • Annual revenue of S$300,000 and above
  • Profitable or near-profitable financials
  • Director(s) with good personal credit score
  • No recent CCJ or bankruptcy history

■ Some banks offer EFS loans backed by Enterprise Singapore with lower bars for newer SMEs.

Private Funder

■ Private Funder loans

  • Singapore-registered business
  • As little as 3–6 months in operation
  • Proof of business income or invoices
  • Director NRIC and personal guarantee
  • No active bankruptcy proceedings
  • Collateral may be requested for larger amounts

■ Private Funders are licensed moneylenders, regulated by MinLaw with interest capped at 4%/month. Verify any Private Funder at moneylenders.registry.sg.

Get Prepared

Documents you'll typically need

Having these ready before you apply speeds up assessment significantly. Exact requirements vary by lender and loan size — your advisor will confirm what's needed for your application.

For businesses (Pte Ltd / LLP)

  • ACRA business profile (recently issued)
  • Last 6–12 months of company bank statements
  • Latest 2 years of financial statements or management accounts
  • Directors' NRIC (front and back)
  • Directors' latest IRAS Notice of Assessment
  • GST F5 returns, if GST-registered

For individuals (personal loans)

  • NRIC (front and back)
  • Latest income documents — payslips or Notice of Assessment
  • CPF contribution history (for salaried applicants)
  • Recent bank statements (for self-employed applicants)
